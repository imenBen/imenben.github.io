I am looking for curious and motivated students who want to do research that matters for people.

## Open topics
* **Human-centered RE, with and for AI** (priority: experience requirements): defining, mining and verifying psychological needs in software. Ideal for students interested in RE, human-computer interaction or psychology.
* **Software safety in the era of agentic AI**: requirements and safety assurance for LLM-based and agentic systems.
* **Quantum software engineering**: quantum optimization for software engineering problems.

See [Research](/research/) for the ongoing projects in each area.

## What I look for
* A degree in software engineering, computer science or a related field
* Strong writing skills in English or French, and the will to improve both
* Interest in empirical methods, from qualitative studies to large-scale data analysis

## How to apply
Send me an email with the subject line **"Prospective [MSc/PhD] student: [project]"**, including your CV, transcripts and one paragraph on the problem you would like to work on and why. I read every message, but I may not be able to reply to generic requests.

ÉTS programs: [MSc and PhD in engineering](https://www.etsmtl.ca/).
