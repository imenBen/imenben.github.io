## Events and service
* **RE'26**, IEEE International Requirements Engineering Conference, ÉTS Montréal: Student Volunteers co-chair and program committee member.
* **HCARE 2026**, workshop on human-centered AI and requirements engineering: co-organizer, with keynote by Jane Cleland-Huang.

## RE Cares
RE Cares brings requirements engineering to community organizations. Our edition partners with **Épilepsie Section de Québec** to understand and support the needs of people living with epilepsy and those around them.
