**The question.** Functional and quality requirements say what a system must do and how well. They rarely say whether the system leaves people feeling competent, autonomous and connected. This project develops requirements engineering that puts people first, both *for* AI systems, which must be specified around human values, and *with* AI, which can help us discover and check those requirements at scale.

**Why it matters.** Apps and AI systems that erode autonomy or wellbeing can satisfy every classic requirement and still fail their users. Making these needs explicit lets teams design for them, test them and be accountable for them.

## Experience requirements

Experience requirements are a new category of requirements that capture how well an interactive system fulfils people's emotional and psychological needs, such as autonomy, competence and relatedness. We define the concept, mine it from real user feedback, and make it verifiable.

* **Experience requirements empirical pipeline:** large-scale extraction and annotation of experience requirements from user feedback. Led by postdoc Narjes Bessghaier, in collaboration with Walid Maalej (University of Hamburg).
* **Psychological needs in app reviews:** studying how users voice need fulfilment and frustration in the wild (ICSE 2027 NIER submission).
* **Human-centered RE mapping study:** a systematic mapping of human-centered requirements engineering (JSS, under revision).
* **User autonomy as verifiable requirements (NOVA):** operationalizing autonomy so that it can be specified and checked.
* **RE Cares with Épilepsie Section de Québec:** applying human-centered RE with a community partner.

## Requirements engineering for AI

How do we specify and elicit requirements for systems built on machine learning and large language models, so that they solve the right problem for the right people?

* **From business problems to AI solutions:** guidance for turning business needs into well-specified AI projects (RE'26), and its follow-up tool ATPAssist (with Abir Trabelsi).
* **Value-based RE for human-centered AI** (RETRAI, with Amel Bennaceur).
* **Telecom fraud detection** with an industrial partner, funded by an NSERC Alliance grant (with Nour Elhouda Boussandel).
