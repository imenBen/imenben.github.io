**The question.** Can quantum optimization help solve hard software engineering problems, and how should we engineer software for fast-moving quantum platforms?

**Why it matters.** Software architecture recovery and similar tasks are combinatorial problems where quantum approaches such as QAOA may offer new trade-offs. At the same time, quantum frameworks evolve quickly, which makes maintainability a research problem in itself.

## Ongoing work

* **QAOA for software architecture recovery**, with Shaukat Ali (Simula / OsloMet) and Ghizlane El Boussaidi (ÉTS), funded by an NSERC Alliance International Catalyst Quantum grant.
* **Migrating quantum code across framework versions** (Qiskit 1.x to 2.x), with Julien Cardinal.
* **Requirements for hybrid optimization loops**, connecting quantum optimizers and AI agents (with Andrew Chedid).
