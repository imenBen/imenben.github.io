**The question.** AI agents now write code, call APIs and take decisions on our behalf. How do we specify what they must never do, detect when they drift, and give evidence that they are safe enough to deploy?

**Why it matters.** Without clear requirements and safety guarantees, agent failures are hard to anticipate and harder to attribute. Software engineering needs new methods to bring safety assurance to systems whose behaviour is learned rather than programmed.

## Ongoing work

* **SafeAgent:** a framework for the safety of coding agents (with Chaima Aouichi).
* **Safety assurance catalog** for autonomous AI systems (with Leonardo Ranaldi).
* **Adaptive agent attacks on APIs** and their defenses (PhD project).
